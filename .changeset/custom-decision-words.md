Decision and correction words can be extended in `~/.agentlore/words.json` (`decisionWords`, `correctionWords`), read on every search. The built-in lists gained everyday Vietnamese ("duyệt", "làm luôn", "đồng ý", "lộn", "ý tôi là", …) and English ("go ahead with", "I meant", …) phrases.
